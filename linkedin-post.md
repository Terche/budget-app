# LinkedIn Post — Peso Tracker

---

Excited to share a personal project I've been building: **Peso Tracker** — a mobile budgeting app made specifically for Filipinos. 🇵🇭

Managing personal finances in the Philippines comes with its own challenges — juggling multiple loans, tracking bi-weekly savings goals, and keeping an eye on spending across different categories. I didn't find an app that felt built *for* that, so I built one.

**Here's what it does:**

💸 **Transactions** — log income and expenses with categories, then filter by date range or multiple categories at once to see exactly where your money goes

📊 **Reports** — responsive pie, bar, and line charts showing expense breakdowns, income vs expenses, and cumulative savings growth over time

🏦 **Debts & Loans** — track every loan (personal, home, car, business) with interest calculations, estimated payoff dates, and a full payment history that updates your remaining balance automatically

🪙 **Bi-Weekly Savings** — set a savings goal and the app generates contribution entries every 14 days to keep you on track

📤 **CSV Export** — share your data to Files, email, or Drive anytime, no account required

Everything is stored locally on the device — no sign-up, no server, no data leaving your phone.

**Built with:**
- Expo (React Native) + TypeScript
- Expo Router for navigation
- React Context + AsyncStorage for local persistence
- react-native-svg for charts
- pnpm workspaces monorepo

The whole thing is open source — link in the comments.

Would love feedback from anyone in the fintech or mobile dev space, or anyone who's been looking for a peso-first budgeting tool.
